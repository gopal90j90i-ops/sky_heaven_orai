Our Journey

A History of Sky Heaven

From a humble kitchen in Orai to the city's most loved dining destination — every year a new chapter.

2012
The First Plate
Sky Heaven opens its doors near Bhagat Singh Churaha with just 8 tables and a menu of 20 dishes. Within weeks, word of the Paneer Tikka spread across Orai.
2015
Expanding the Kitchen
Growing demand led to a full kitchen expansion, new tandoor, and the introduction of our Dal Makhani recipe that's still unchanged to this day.
2019
Chinese Cuisine & Fast Food
We added a dedicated Indo-Chinese counter — Schezwan Noodles, Chilli Paneer, and Fried Rice became instant hits with college students and families alike.
2022
Online Ordering
Sky Heaven went digital — launching online ordering via Zomato and our own platform, serving hundreds of daily orders across Orai Locality.
2024
685+ Happy Diners & Going Strong
With a 4.0 rating and 685+ reviews, Sky Heaven continues to be Orai's most trusted name in dining. More locations coming soon!
